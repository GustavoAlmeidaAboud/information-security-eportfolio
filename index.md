## Module learning outcomes

1. Identify and critically analyse security risks, threats and vulnerabilities in information systems, taking the current threat landscape into account.
2. Gather and synthesise information from multiple sources (including internet security alerts and warning sites) to support systematic analysis of risks and security issues.
3. Critically determine appropriate methodologies, tools and techniques to mitigate or solve security risks and their business impact.
4. Articulate the legal, social, ethical and professional issues faced by information security and risk professionals.
